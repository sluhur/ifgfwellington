Doctrine

What we believe

  1. We believe the Bible (Old and New Testaments) is inspired by the Holy Spirit and is fully authoritative as originally given. (2 Timothy 3:16; 1 Corinthians 2:13)
  2. We believe in one eternal God, revealed as three distinct but inseparable persons: Father, Son and Holy Spirit. (Matthew 28:19; 2 Corinthians 13:13)
  3. We believe in the creation, temptation and fall of the first man as recorded in Genesis, and in his spiritual death and inability to attain divine righteousness. (Genesis 3:1-19; Romans 5:12,18)
  4. We believe in the Lord Jesus Christ as fully God and fully man, the Savior of mankind, conceived by the Holy Spirit and born of the virgin Mary. (John 1:1,14; Matthew 1:18-21; Luke 1:26-35, 2:10-11; Isaiah 7:14)
  5. We believe that Jesus Christ died for the sins of mankind, was buried, rose again on the third day, and personally appeared to His disciples. (1 Corinthians 15:14; John 20:19,26; 21:1; Romans 4:25)
  6. We believe in the bodily ascension of Jesus into heaven, His glory, and His personal, bodily return for His church. (John 14:2-3; 1 Thessalonians 4:13-18)
  7. We believe sinners are saved by God's grace through repentance and faith in the perfect work of Jesus Christ at Calvary, through which we also receive forgiveness of our sins. (Romans 5:11; Ephesians 1:7, 2:8-9; Hebrews 9:12)
  8. We believe in water baptism by immersion in the name of the Father, the Son and the Holy Spirit, in the name of the Lord Jesus Christ, in obedience to Christ's command. (Matthew 28:19; Acts 2:34-36; 19:1-6)
  9. We believe in the baptism of the Holy Spirit as an essential experience after salvation, with the biblical evidence of speaking in tongues as the Spirit gives utterance. (Acts 2:1-4; 8:14-17; 10:44-46; Galatians 3:14-15)
  10. We believe in the gifts of the Holy Spirit and their operation as described in 1 Corinthians 12-14, as experienced by the early church.
  11. We believe in a Spirit-filled life, set apart from worldliness and lived in holiness and the fear of God as an expression of Christian faith. (2 Corinthians 6:14; 7:1; Ephesians 5:18)
  12. We believe in divine healing of body and mind, worked by the power of God in various ways, as in the early church. (Mark 16:17-18; Acts 4:30; Romans 8:11; 1 Corinthians 12:9; James 5:14)
  13. We believe in the Lord's Table, commonly called Holy Communion, for believers. (1 Corinthians 11:28-32)
  14. We believe in eternal life for every believer. (John 5:24; 5:16) We also believe in eternal punishment for every unbeliever. (Mark 9:43-58; 2 Thessalonians 1:9; Revelation 20:10-15)
  15. We believe in the existence and work of Satan, and in the eternal punishment of Satan and his angels. (Matthew 25:41; Revelation 20:10-15)
